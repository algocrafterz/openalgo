WHICH RULES ACTUALLY CARRY IT

  "Select only Top Gainers" - THE most important rule, and it is cross-sectional.
    Ranking the whole universe and taking the top 5 is what makes this work:
    top-3 +0.213 R, top-5 +0.140, top-10 -0.035, top-20 -0.045, no rank -0.011.
    An absolute threshold ("up more than 2%") is not a substitute for the rank.
  "Wait for a healthy pullback" - load-bearing. Removing it takes OOS from
    +0.146 to -0.011 R. The 20-70% retrace band is where it works.
  "Volume should support the breakout" - helps. Removing it: +0.140 -> +0.042 R.
  "Look for a Bullish Engulfing / Hammer / Rejection" - INERT. Results are
    bit-identical with confirmation off, because a bullish close in the top 30%
    of its range describes almost every breakout bar. It is not filtering
    anything. Trust the pullback and the volume, not the candle.
  "Buy after price breaks the First Day High" - on its own, a LOSER. With no
    pullback, confirmation or volume requirement it is -0.012 R (113 trades),
    negative out-of-sample. The FDH break is the trigger, never the edge.

TARGETS. The 1:2 / 1:3 rungs barely matter: 69% of trades reach neither the
target nor the stop and exit on the clock, with a median exit of +0.14 R. This
is a slow drift edge, not a target-hitting edge. Expectancy across 1.0R to 3.0R
targets is flat (+0.15 to +0.20 R), so "Target 1 at 1:2" is fine but is not
where the money is - staying in a strong gainer is.
