Intraday loser short (owner's approved hypothesis, candidate A, 2026-10-07). DRAFT: not frozen, not run.

Hypothesis: on each session, short the 10 eligible F&O stocks with the lowest mean intraday
return over the previous 60 eligible sessions, where a stock's intraday return
r = close of the 14:45 bar / open of the 09:20 bar - 1. That basket earns a higher gross
09:20-to-14:45 short return than (a) shorting random other eligible stocks on the same sessions
and (b) the unconditional universe short, and it clears costs.

Rationale: tug-of-war clientele (Lou, Polk, Skouras 2019; Heston, Korajczyk, Sadka 2010):
retail/attention buying at or before the open, long-biased MIS books unwound through the day,
institutions on the other side; the universe's strongly negative NSE intraday drift is this
effect at market level; the question is whether it is concentrated in identifiable stocks.

Proposed rules (one pre-specified parameter set, no grids):
- Universe = published F&O universe per date.
- Eligibility = at least 60 valid prior sessions, not a split/bonus session, the platform price floor.
- Signal m60 = mean r over the previous 60 sessions (sd60 = its standard deviation).
- Select the 10 lowest m60, ties alphabetical.
- Entry short at the 09:20 bar open.
- Exit at the standing time exit (close of the 14:45 bar, per the Flattrade profile).
- Protective stop at entry x (1 + 1.5 x sd60).
- Rs 1,00,000 fixed notional per position, at most 10 concurrent, one trade per symbol per day, no re-entry.
- Dataset nse-intraday-5m-20261006-001; product MIS; cost primary = profile broker (Flattrade)
  statutory per-trade model; costs: {reference_round_trip_bps: [32]} as a stress reference line.

Falsification, decided now (any one kills it):
- Cross-sectional same-session excess over random other eligible stocks (baseline_cross_sectional,
  pending implementation) <= 0 or day-clustered excess t < 2.0. A pass from drift alone is recorded
  as "drift harvesting, selection adds nothing", not an edge.
- Net fails G1.2 (+0.05R) or net t < 2.5.
- Net <= 0 at 2x slippage (G1.3).
- OOS (from 2023-11-01, same convention as IDEA-029) net <= 0 or < 40% of IS.
- Positive in fewer than 6 of 10 years 2017-2026 or worst year < -0.25R.
- Top-10 symbols > 40% of net profit or < 50% of traded symbols net positive.

Pre-declared diagnostics (report only): decile monotonicity of m60 vs next-day r; the mirror
top-10 long leg; entry delayed one bar; the random-short baseline by year.

Information boundary: m60 and sd60 from sessions up to the previous session's 14:45 bar; at 09:20
only the eligibility check; no same-day information in the ranking.
