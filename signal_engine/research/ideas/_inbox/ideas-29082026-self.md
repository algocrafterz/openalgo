VWAP 2SD Mean-Reversion Strategy
Timeframes
- 15-min: identify range
- 5-min: entry
  Indicators
- Session VWAP
- VWAP ±2SD
- ATR(14)
- ADX(14)
  Setup
1. Range: ADX < 22, VWAP/20EMA relatively flat.
2. Extreme: Price reaches ±2SD and is at least 0.75 ATR from VWAP.
3. Rejection: Price closes back inside the ±2SD band.
4. Entry: Break of rejection candle.
5. SL: Rejection high/low ± 0.2 ATR.
6. Target: VWAP.
   Long: -2SD rejection → buy → VWAP.
   Short: +2SD rejection → short → VWAP.
   Avoid: Strong trend days, steep VWAP slope, ADX > 22.
   Core idea: Trade failed extremes back toward VWAP, not simply touches of the 2SD bands.

|---|---|

| **SD Bands** | ±2.0 SD |
| **ATR** | ATR(14) |
| **ADX** | ADX(14) < 22 |

| **Entry timeframe** | 5-min |
