## Three questions, kept apart

| Question | Answered by | Possible answers |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Research validity: can the data and the simulation support any conclusion? | integrity gates, the validity block, data quality | PASS, FAIL, BLOCKED |
| Strategy evidence: is there a credible edge after costs? | strategy-quality and risk gates | EDGE, PROMISING, MARGINAL, NO_EDGE, INCONCLUSIVE |
| Promotion readiness: may the strategy move to the next stage? | stage decision, limitations, dirty tree, reproducibility | READY_FOR_NEXT_STAGE, NOT_READY, BLOCKED |

A missing prerequisite is BLOCKED and too little data is INCONCLUSIVE; neither is a failure of the
strategy and neither is ever a pass. A failed integrity gate makes the verdict INCONCLUSIVE, because
the simulation, not the strategy, is what failed.

## Gate classes


- **integrity**: Research-integrity prerequisite: data, causality or execution assumptions. A failure invalidates every conclusion that depends on it.
- **strategy_quality**: Strategy-quality test: edge, significance, out-of-sample stability, robustness.
- **risk_constraint**: Risk constraint: drawdown, ruin, sizing, capacity and margin at the deployed size.
- **promotion_operational**: Promotion or operational requirement: checked at the paper or live stage.

## Why a gate reads "not evaluated"

- **stage_not_run**: BLOCKED: the opt-in evidence stage has not been run (or its evidence was rejected). Run the stage named in the next action.
- **attestation**: BLOCKED: no valid attestation covers this run. Record one for the exact commit.
- **sample**: INCONCLUSIVE: the evidence exists but is too small to decide (trade, window or symbol count below the rule's floor). More data, not a different strategy.
- **structural**: BLOCKED: cannot be resolved by a backtest. It needs a later stage, an external input or an owner decision.
- **data_prerequisite**: BLOCKED: an input the gate reads is missing from the run record (often a record written before the input existed).
